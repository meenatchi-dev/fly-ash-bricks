Who We Are

Redefining Industrial Brick Manufacturing Standards

At Meenakshi Flyash Bricks, we manufacture premium fly ash bricks using automated hydraulic press systems. Our mission is to produce building materials that exceed Indian Standards (IS 12894) while preserving nature by recycling fly ash.
