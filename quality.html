Laboratory Tested

Engineered to Exceed Industry Standards

Our bricks are tested internally and third-party certified to ensure they safely withstand structural load requirements and environmental weathering.

IS Conformance

Manufactured in strict compliance with Bureau of Indian Standards code IS 12894:2002 (Fly Ash Lime Bricks Specification).

Comp. Strength

Average compressive strength exceeds 10 to 12.5 N/mm², which is 2x stronger than standard local red clay bricks.

Water Absorption

Stops water penetration. Water absorption rates remain below 8% to 10%, preventing dampness and salt efflorescence.

Eco-Friendly

Earns maximum points for LEED & IGBC green ratings. Contains up to 70% recycled industrial fly ash materials.

Fly Ash Bricks vs. Traditional Clay Bricks

See why civil engineers and developers are shifting away from red clay bricks to engineered fly ash blocks.

Property / Feature Meenakshi Flyash Bricks Fly Ash Traditional Clay Bricks
Compressive Strength > 10 N/mm² (Highly Stable) 3.5 to 5 N/mm² (Varies)
Dimensional Uniformity Highly Uniform (± 1mm variance) Highly Uneven (± 5mm variance)
Mortar Consumption Saves up to 20% (Flat surfaces) High consumption (To cover unevenness)
