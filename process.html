How We Build It

Fully Automated Production Line

Our plant utilizes automated batching, high-pressure hydraulic compaction, and steam curing chambers to secure consistent density and class strength.

Step 01

Raw Material Proportions

We weigh raw fly ash (60-70%), sand/quarry dust (20-25%), cement/lime (8-10%), and gypsum (2-5%) using computerized load-cell hoppers. This ensures absolute chemical conformity across batches.

Step 02

Homogeneous Mixing

The materials are transferred to double-shaft pan mixers. Water is introduced via flow-meters to achieve optimal moisture distribution. Dry and wet mixing continues until a uniform composite paste forms.

Step 03

High Pressure Hydraulic Pressing

The wet paste enters high-precision steel molds. An automated hydraulic press compresses the mixture under a pressure of 150 to 200 tonnes, locking the materials into a dense, solid brick matrix with clean, square edges.

Step 04

Controlled Steam Curing

Bricks are stacked on pallets and loaded into steam curing chambers. They are cured at 80°C with high humidity for 24-48 hours. This accelerates the hydration of cement, allowing bricks to gain full load strength rapidly.

Step 05

Quality Control Inspections

Sample units from each cure run are sent to our laboratory. We check for dimensional accuracy (±1mm variance max), compressive crush tolerance, and water absorption rates to satisfy IS standards.

Step 06

Stacking Yards & Site Dispatch

Approved brick batches are stacked on wooden pallets and shrink-wrapped. Forklifts load the pallets onto our transport trucks for dispatch directly to building sites, preventing manual damage.
